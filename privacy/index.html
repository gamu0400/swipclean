Privacy Policy

Swipeclean · Effective October 7, 2026

In short: your photos and videos never leave your phone. Swipeclean has no servers, no accounts and uploads nothing. The only internet connection is used to show one ad on the final screen, through Google AdMob.

1. Who is responsible

Swipeclean is developed by Bane. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at gonzalo000415@gmail.com.

2. Your photos and videos

Swipeclean shows you your photos and videos so you can decide which to keep and which to delete. All of this happens on your phone:

  • We do not copy, upload or share your photos or videos with anyone, including us.
  • The details shown for each photo (date, size, album, camera model, etc.) are read from the file on your phone and never leave it.
  • We do not read the GPS location of your photos.
  • Nothing is deleted without your confirmation. Deleted items first go to the Android trash, where you can restore them for 30 days (Android 11 or later).

3. Permissions the app uses

PermissionWhy
Photos and videos
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED		 To show your photos and videos so you can sort them. You can grant access to only some of them.
Storage (Android 12 or earlier)
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 10 or earlier only)		 The same on older Android versions, and to delete what you confirm (those versions have no trash).
Media management (optional)
MANAGE_MEDIA		 If you enable it in Settings, items you choose are moved to the trash without an extra Android confirmation dialog. You can turn it off at any time.
Internet
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE		 Only to show the ad. Your photos are never sent over the internet.
Advertising ID
AD_ID		 Used by Google AdMob for ads (see section 5).

4. What is stored on your phone

So you don't lose your progress, the app stores only on your phone:

  • Which photos you have reviewed and what you decided for each one (the photo's internal number is stored, never the photo).
  • Your preferences, for example whether you have seen the tutorial.

This information is not sent anywhere and is completely deleted when you uninstall the app or use “Clear data” in Android Settings.

5. Ads (Google AdMob)

Swipeclean shows a single ad, on the final screen, through Google AdMob. To show and measure it and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • The device advertising ID and other device identifiers.
  • IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred).
  • Information about your interaction with the ad, and technical and diagnostic data.

This data is processed by Google under its own policy. Learn more at “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services” and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent: where required by law (for example, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before loading any ad, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice from “Ad privacy” on the final screen.

Limiting personalized ads: on your phone, go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and delete or reset your advertising ID.

6. Children

Swipeclean is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Security

Because your photos never leave your phone, there are no copies on any server that could leak. Ad communication with Google is encrypted.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new date.
